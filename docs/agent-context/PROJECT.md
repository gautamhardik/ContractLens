# ContractLens

## Purpose
ContractLens is an intelligent contract review and obligation tracking agent designed to convert dense, static commercial agreements into queryable, structured operational intelligence with verifiable source attribution.

## Locked Problem Statement
**PS4 — ContractLens: Business Contract Review & Obligation Tracking Agent**

## Core Intended Capabilities
- **Contract Ingestion**: Robust text and structure extraction from complex commercial agreements and amendments.
- **Key Information Extraction**: Automated identification of parties, effective dates, governing laws, payment terms, and critical definitions.
- **Obligation & Milestone Extraction**: Identification and classification of operational duties, deliverables, compliance requirements, and covenants.
- **Deadline & Renewal Tracking**: Timeline extraction of expiration dates, renewal windows, notice deadlines, and termination rights.
- **Contract Q&A with Evidence**: Grounded answering of user inquiries backed by verbatim clause citations and page-level provenance.
- **Contract Comparison & Amendment Tracking**: Analysis of parent agreements versus amendments, superseding clauses, and version deltas.
- **Risk & Review Flags**: Automated detection of non-standard, aggressive, or ambiguous clauses (e.g., unlimited liability, auto-renewal trap).
- **Natural-Language Investigation**: Guided investigation of contract portfolios for specific business or legal queries.

## Product Principle
ContractLens transforms static business contracts into actionable operational intelligence while maintaining verifiable source fidelity.

## Hackathon Constraints
- **Timeline**: 2-day rapid hackathon prototype.
- **Focus**: High-impact prototype demonstrating reliable AI integration, transparent source citations, and clear UX.
- **Verifiable Accuracy**: Hallucination-free extraction; every contractual assertion must trace back to document evidence.
